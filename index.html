Why people switch to TrialGuard

No bank connection

Track trials by entering them (or forwarding the confirmation email). We never ask for banking credentials — the #1 fear in every subscription-app thread.

No pay-to-cancel

Reminders and cancellation guides are free. We don't charge you a subscription to escape other subscriptions.

Exact cancellation paths

Step-by-step guides for tricky cases — like subscriptions billed through a third platform (Roku, Amazon, app stores) where the "cancel" button hides.

Works outside the US

Manual entry means any country, any bank, any currency. No US-only bank integrations required.
